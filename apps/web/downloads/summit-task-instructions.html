Summit Tasks

Transform your to-do list into an adventure. Organize tasks by category, complete them, and earn badges as you reach new heights.

How to Use

1

Add Your Tasks

Type a task in the input field, select a category (Work, Personal, or Shopping), and click Add Task.

2

Organize by Category

Your tasks automatically sort into three columns. Keep your work, personal, and shopping items in separate trails.

3

Check Off Progress

Click the circle next to any task to mark it complete. Watch your progress bar climb and badges unlock.

4

Earn Achievements

Complete 1, 3, 6, or 10 tasks to unlock First Steps, On a Roll, Peak Performer, and Summit Master badges.
